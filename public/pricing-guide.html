Hair Service Pricing Guide

Price your services so every job pays your staff, covers your bills and still leaves you a profit. Works for any mix: ventilation, wigmaking, revamping, installation, styling, or anything else you offer.

The numbers already filled in are examples. Replace them with yours, delete the services you don't offer and add your own. Your entries stay saved in this browser.

Direct cost

What one job uses up: lace, thread, products, packaging, tool wear.

Commission

What you pay the person who does the job, as a % of the price.

Running costs

Rent, light, fuel, salaries, marketing. Every job pays its share.

Profit

What is left for you after all three. Aim for 40–50%.
